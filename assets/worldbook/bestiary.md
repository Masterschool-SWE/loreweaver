# Bestiary of Aethelgard

Each entry lists the creature's **challenge rating (CR)** — compare it to a hero's level. The same
creatures are seeded into the game database's `monsters` table.

## Cinderwyrm
- **Type:** Dragon
- **Challenge Rating:** 12
- **HP:** 200
- **Abilities:** Fire breath (cone), molten scales (melee attackers take burn damage), flight.
- **Lore:** Born in Emberhold's lava tubes, the Cinderwyrm hoards not gold but heat — it nests on
  beds of burning coal. It is vulnerable to cold magic and to the Tide Shard's water. Frostmere
  hunters prize its still-warm scales.

## Frostmaw
- **Type:** Beast
- **Challenge Rating:** 6
- **HP:** 90
- **Abilities:** Freezing bite, ice-step (silent on snow), pack tactics.
- **Lore:** A great white wolf of Frostmere that hunts in pairs. Frostmaws are intelligent enough
  to drive prey toward cliffs. They will not cross running water.

## Gloomtide Kraken
- **Type:** Aberration
- **Challenge Rating:** 15
- **HP:** 300
- **Abilities:** Tentacle grapple, ink cloud (blinds), pull-under (drowning).
- **Lore:** The terror of the Sunken Coast. The Tideborn leave it offerings of bronze so it sleeps.
  It despises fire and recoils from open flame, the one weakness Emberhold raiders exploit.

## Ashwalker
- **Type:** Undead
- **Challenge Rating:** 4
- **HP:** 45
- **Abilities:** Life-drain touch, immune to fear, reforms unless its anchor-bone is shattered.
- **Lore:** The walking dead of the Ashen Waste. An Ashwalker is bound to a single bone hidden
  somewhere on its body; destroy the anchor-bone and it crumbles for good. Holy fire and the Void
  Shard's silence both undo them.

## Stormtalon
- **Type:** Beast
- **Challenge Rating:** 8
- **HP:** 110
- **Abilities:** Lightning dive, gale wings (knockback), keen sight from a mile up.
- **Lore:** The giant griffons of Highreach. Tamed Stormtalons carry riders; wild ones are
  territorial and strike from the sun. They fear nothing but the Cinderwyrm.

## Mirewight
- **Type:** Fey
- **Challenge Rating:** 2
- **HP:** 20
- **Abilities:** Illusion (false paths), drowning lullaby, blends into bog water.
- **Lore:** A mischievous spirit of the Verdant Reach's swamps. Mostly harmless, but it leads the
  unwary in circles. A song of apology, sung aloud, sends it away laughing.

## Hollow King
- **Type:** Undead (boss)
- **Challenge Rating:** 18
- **HP:** 400
- **Abilities:** Command undead, shard-siphon (drains magic), unkillable while the Seventh Shard is
  unfound.
- **Lore:** A rumor more than a creature — said to rule the Hollow Heart from a throne of broken
  roads, waiting for the lost Seventh Shard. No verified party has faced him and returned. Treat
  any claim of a Hollow King sighting with deep suspicion.
