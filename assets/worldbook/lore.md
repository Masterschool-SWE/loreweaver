# The Realm of Aethelgard — Lore

## Origins

Aethelgard is a fractured continent born from the shattering of the **First Star**, an event the
chroniclers call the **Sundering**. When the star fell, its light split into seven shards that
buried themselves across the land. Each shard radiates a different kind of magic, and the regions
that grew around them took on the character of their shard.

## The Seven Regions

### Emberhold
A volcanic highland in the south, lit by the **Crimson Shard**. Its people, the Emberkin, are
smiths and firespeakers. The capital, **Cinderspire**, is built into the throat of a dormant
volcano. Fire magic is strongest here, and weakest underwater.

### The Verdant Reach
A vast forest fed by the **Green Shard**. Druids and beast-wardens keep the old pacts with the
woodland spirits. Outsiders who cut living wood without offering a song are said to be led in
circles until they apologize.

### Frostmere
A frozen north under the pale **Silver Shard**. The city of **Hollowfrost** sits on a lake that
never fully thaws. Frostmere scholars keep the deepest libraries in Aethelgard, because cold
preserves old paper.

### The Sunken Coast
Drowned ruins along the western sea, claimed by the **Tide Shard**. Tidecallers can breathe water
for an hour at a time. Many pre-Sundering relics still rest beneath the waves, guarded by things
that do not love the surface.

### The Ashen Waste
A grey desert in the east where the **Void Shard** fell. Magic behaves strangely here — spells
sometimes echo, sometimes fail entirely. The Waste is the only place the dead are said to walk by
daylight.

### Highreach
A range of sky-piercing peaks holding the **Storm Shard**. Highreach griffon-riders carry messages
between the regions. Lightning never strikes the same tower twice — the towers are built to make
sure of it.

### The Hollow Heart
The center of the continent, where the **Seventh Shard** has never been found. The Hollow Heart is
a no-man's-land of broken roads and abandoned waystations. Every faction wants the lost shard;
none will admit to looking for it.

## The Factions

- **The Lantern Order** — keepers of roads and safe passage. Neutral, trusted, slow to anger.
- **The Crimson Forge** — Emberhold's smith-guild; the finest weapons in Aethelgard bear their mark.
- **The Verdant Circle** — druids who answer to the forest, not to kings.
- **The Pale Archive** — Frostmere's scholars, hoarders of forbidden knowledge.
- **The Tideborn** — secretive salvagers of the Sunken Coast.
- **The Ashen Few** — survivors of the Waste, distrusted everywhere, loyal only to each other.

## A Note on the Shards

A shard cannot be destroyed, only moved. Carrying a shard for too long changes a person to match
its nature — the bearer of the Crimson Shard runs a fever they never lose; the bearer of the Silver
Shard stops feeling the cold, and eventually stops feeling much at all. Wise adventurers deliver
shards quickly and do not ask to hold them.
