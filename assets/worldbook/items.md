# Items of Aethelgard

The same items are seeded into the game database's `items` table. Value is in gold; selling returns
half (see rules).

## Weapons

### Cinderbrand (Legendary, value 5000)
A longsword forged in Cinderspire from a sliver of the Crimson Shard. Its blade is always warm and
sheds light like a torch. Deals extra burn damage; especially effective against the Gloomtide
Kraken and other cold-loving foes. Only one exists.

### Frostfang Dagger (Epic, value 1200)
A Frostmere blade that never dulls and leaves frost on every wound. Favored by rogues for the
**Backstab** ability. Effective against the Cinderwyrm.

### Stormbow (Rare, value 600)
A Highreach longbow strung with griffon sinew. Arrows loosed from it crackle with static and rarely
miss in open air. A ranger's prize.

### Iron Shortsword (Common, value 25)
A plain, reliable blade from any village smithy. Nothing special, but it never lets you down.

## Armor

### Aegis of the Lantern (Epic, value 1500)
A shield blessed by the Lantern Order. Glows softly in darkness and turns aside the first blow of
any fight. Pairs with a warrior's **Shield Wall**.

### Tideweave Cloak (Rare, value 700)
Woven by the Tideborn from sea-silk. Lets the wearer breathe water for one hour and dries instantly.
Essential for diving the Sunken Coast.

### Leather Jerkin (Common, value 20)
Basic protection. Light, quiet, and cheap — a rogue's first armor.

## Trinkets & Consumables

### Shardglass Lens (Rare, value 800)
A lens ground from inert shard-dust. Reveals hidden illusions — see through a Mirewight's false
paths or an Ashwalker's disguise.

### Healing Draught (Uncommon, value 50)
A red potion brewed by the Verdant Circle. Restores a third of a hero's maximum HP. Stackable.

### Griffon Whistle (Uncommon, value 120)
Summons a tamed Stormtalon from the nearest Highreach roost — if one is within range and willing.

### Waystone (Common, value 10)
A palm-sized marker stone. Drop one to remember a location; the Keeper will recall it later.
